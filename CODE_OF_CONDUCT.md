# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Everyone who contributes to pytrackio — regardless of background, experience level,
or identity — deserves to be treated with respect.

## Our Standards

**Expected behaviour:**
- Be kind and respectful in all interactions
- Give constructive feedback, not personal criticism
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Help beginners — everyone starts somewhere

**Unacceptable behaviour:**
- Harassment or personal attacks of any kind
- Dismissing contributions because of someone's skill level
- Discriminatory language or behaviour

## Enforcement

Issues can be reported by opening a GitHub issue or contacting the maintainer directly.
Anyone violating these standards may be blocked from contributing.
